Frequently Asked Questions

Everything gym owners, store owners and distributors ask before placing their first order with us.

Ordering, Samples & MOQ

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our minimum order quantity depends on the product — typically one dozen for accessories like hand wraps, and up to a few dozen pairs for boxing gloves or head guards. Send us your product list and target quantity and we'll confirm the exact MOQ with your quote.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes, we strongly recommend it. We produce a sample for your approval first, so you can check the fit, material and finish before committing to a full production run.

What is the typical lead time for a bulk order?

Sample production usually takes 2–3 days once details are confirmed. Bulk production time depends on order size and is confirmed with your quotation, so you can plan your stock arrival with confidence.

Do you work with gyms and retail stores, or only large distributors?

Both. We supply everyone from individual gym owners and sports stores placing their first wholesale order to established distributors and retail chains ordering container loads.

Private Labeling & Customization

Can you put our own brand, logo and packaging on the products?

Yes. Private labeling is one of our core services. We can add your logo, choose your colours, and pack every product in your own branded packaging, so it reaches your customers as your brand, not ours.

Do you make fully custom designs, or only your standard catalogue?

Both. You can order from our standard range, or send us a sketch, reference photo or spec sheet and our team will develop a sample to your exact design before bulk production begins.

What materials do you use?

We work with genuine leather, PU leather, canvas, high-density shock foam and cotton, depending on the product and your budget. We can also match a specific material or grade if you have one in mind.

Payment, Shipping & Trust

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer (T/T), Western Union, MoneyGram and Ria for samples and smaller orders, and Letter of Credit (L/C) for large bulk export orders. Terms are confirmed on your quotation.

How do you ship orders, and how long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched from Sialkot via DHL or UPS for air shipments, or sea freight for large bulk orders. Air delivery typically takes 7–15 days and sea freight longer, depending on your country; we confirm exact timelines with your quote.

Are you a registered, verifiable manufacturer?

Yes. Infinitum Boxing is registered with the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry and the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) of Pakistan, and every order is invoiced properly for your records.

How do I place an order or get pricing?

Fill in our Get a Quote form with your product, quantity and any branding requirements, or contact us directly by phone, WhatsApp or email. We reply with pricing and lead times within 24 hours.

Still have a question?

We reply personally to every message — ask us anything about your order.
