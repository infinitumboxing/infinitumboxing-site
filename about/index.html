About Infinitum Boxing

Infinitum Boxing is a manufacturer and exporter of boxing equipment and MMA wear based in Sialkot, Pakistan. We produce in our own production unit and supply buyers in the US, UK and Europe.

Our business is bank verified, registered with Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and registered with FBR (NTN).
