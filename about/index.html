About Infinitum Boxing

Infinitum Boxing is a boxing gear manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan. We make gloves, head guards and MMA wear in our own factory and supply gym owners, retailers and distributors around the world.

Made in our own factory in Sialkot

Sialkot has been a centre of sports goods manufacturing for generations, and Infinitum Boxing is part of that tradition. Our brand, Fighting Gear & Wear, is built around one idea: fight gear should be made properly, priced fairly and delivered on time.

We run our own production unit, so we control every step ourselves, from cutting the leather to the final quality check. That means consistent quality, honest lead times and no middlemen between you and the people who make your order.

We work mainly with buyers in the USA, the UK and Europe, and we are used to the documentation, packing and communication that international orders need.
