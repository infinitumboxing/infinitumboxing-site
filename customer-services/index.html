Customer Services

Beyond stock products, we work as an extension of your brand — from your logo on the glove to the box it ships in.

Private Labeling

Sell under your own name. Every glove, guard or pad can carry your logo, your colours and your packaging — your customers never see ours.

  • Your logo woven, printed or embossed on the product
  • Custom colourways to match your brand
  • Branded neck tags, boxes and poly bags
  • Available on most products, subject to MOQ

Custom Branding & Logo Printing

Screen printing, embroidery, heat-transfer or debossed leather — we match the technique to the product and the look you want.

  • Screen printing & heat-transfer on fabric and leather
  • Embroidery for a premium, durable finish
  • Debossed or embossed branding on leather goods
  • Colour-matched to your brand palette

OEM Manufacturing

Have your own design? Send a sketch, spec sheet or reference sample and we'll build it exactly to your requirement, not ours.

  • Product development from your sketch or spec
  • Sample produced for your approval before bulk
  • Pattern, material and construction matched to spec
  • Full production once the sample is signed off

Bulk Wholesale Supply

Stock your gym or store from our standard range at factory-direct wholesale pricing, with consistent quality on every batch.

  • Factory-direct pricing at volume
  • Consistent sizing and quality, batch to batch
  • Mixed-product orders welcome
  • Ongoing restock support for regular customers
