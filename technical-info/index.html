HOW THE GEAR GETS MADE

Materials, manufacturing and quality checks

The right product starts with clear specifications. Discuss material, construction, branding and packaging before sample approval and bulk production.

Materials and construction

Material choice depends on the product, intended use and target price. Options discussed across the range include leather, PU leather, canvas, foam and fabrics. Stitching, padding, closures and finishing should all be part of the written product brief.

Quality control

Checks can be aligned to the agreed specification and product. The production process includes reviewing incoming materials, construction and stitching, sample approval and final inspection before packing.

  • Material and colour review
  • Stitching and finishing inspection
  • Sample sign-off before bulk production
  • Final product and packing review

Registration and documentation

Infinitum Boxing is registered with the Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry and Pakistan's Federal Board of Revenue. Confirm the documents and export details required for your order before production.
