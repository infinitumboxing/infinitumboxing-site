BUYER GUIDE / FIT AND USE

Boxing glove sizing for gyms and teams

Glove weight is only one part of fit. Match the range to the training use, gym rules, athlete needs and the exact product construction before you place a team or retail order.

Start with the training use

Boxing gloves are commonly grouped by weight in ounces. Lighter options are often selected for bag work, while heavier gloves are common for sparring. Gym rules, coach direction and athlete size all matter, so treat these as starting points rather than universal prescriptions.

WeightTypical buying context
8–10 ozBag work or lighter glove ranges
12–14 ozGeneral-purpose training
16 ozCommon sparring range
18–20 ozHeavier training requirements

Check the fit beyond the number

Two gloves at the same ounce rating can feel different. Ask about hand compartment fit, wrist closure, thumb construction, padding feel and whether the intended users will wear hand wraps underneath.

Plan a useful gym range

For a club or retailer, record the sizes and weights your members request most often, the rules used by your coaches and the replacement frequency. A balanced range is easier to maintain than ordering a single weight for every use.

  • Separate bag-training and sparring needs
  • Confirm wrist closure preferences
  • Review colours, logo placement and size labels
  • Sample the intended construction before bulk approval

What to send a manufacturer

Include target weights, intended use, preferred outer material, padding feel, closure, logo files, size breakdown, quantity and destination. Those details help the factory respond with a product-specific quotation and sample plan.
